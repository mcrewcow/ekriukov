North star

A quantitative language for cell fate

My goal is to understand when, how, and how strongly cells commit to identity — and turn that knowledge into strategies for retinal development, disease modeling, and regeneration.

Working definition

Cell-fate commitment

The progressive restriction of a cell’s plausible future identities as its molecular state stabilizes along a developmental or response trajectory.

The computational challenge: measure that restriction from high-dimensional single-cell data rather than treating cell identity as a static label.
